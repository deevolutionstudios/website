Hopz

A competitive strategy game with a twist!

About the Game

We had the chance to reimagine a classic with Hopz, Alien Hopz, and Caribbean Hops — a series of competitive strategy games designed and produced for a client.

These games take the simple concept of Tic-Tac-Toe and flip it on its head. Instead of just placing pieces, players hop over opponents to reach new spots, adding layers of movement, strategy, and surprising depth. Every match becomes a fast-paced battle of positioning, forcing players to think ahead and block their opponent while planning their next hop.

Each version brings a unique setting and style — from colorful alien worlds to sun-soaked Caribbean islands — all while keeping the core gameplay tight, fun, and easy to pick up.

The client loved how this twist turned a familiar game into something fresh, competitive, and packed with replayability. For us, it was the perfect project — simple to learn, hard to master, and endlessly fun to play. This is exactly what we love doing: taking an idea and pushing it further until it's something players can't stop playing.
