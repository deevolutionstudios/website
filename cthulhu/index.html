Lil' Cthulhu

You cannot win, you can only survive!

About the Game

Lil' Cthulhu is a fast-paced card game of madness, betrayal, and survival for 2 to 5 players. In this darkly fun game, you play as cultists desperately trying to appease the adorable but terrifying Lil' Cthulhu — a pint-sized Great Old One with a very short temper.

Each round, players scramble to offer gifts, complete rituals, and sabotage their fellow cultists. The goal? Keep Lil' Cthulhu entertained… and keep yourself sane. Fail, and you'll be the next sacrifice.

The game blends strategy, take-that mechanics, and a dose of dark humor. Players must balance self-preservation with clever timing to throw others under the bus — because in the end, there are no winners, only survivors.

Originally funded on Kickstarter, Lil' Cthulhu was created by De-Evolution Studios for anyone who loves card games with chaotic energy, a Lovecraftian twist, and backstabbing fun packed into every turn.
