✦ Account & data deletion

Delete your Colorwise data

Colorwise · by De-Evolution Studios (DE-EVOLUTION STUDIOS LLC)

You only have an account if you signed in to the world leaderboards (with your email, Google or Apple). Playing without signing in needs no account. Your De-Evolution account is shared with our other games that use the same sign-in, such as Candi's Swamp Hop.

How to ask for deletion

  1. Email contact@deevolutionstudios.com from the email address you signed in with (for Sign in with Apple's “Hide My Email”, from the Apple ID that address forwards to), with the subject “Colorwise: delete my account”.
  2. Say whether you want the whole account deleted (for every game that uses it) or only your Colorwise information.
  3. We check that the request comes from the account’s address, delete what you asked for and confirm by email within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your player name and your place on the leaderboards
  • Your best scores on every track
  • Your Prisms, skins and chosen look saved with your account
  • For a whole-account deletion: your sign-in (email address and the Google or Apple sign-in link)

What is kept, and for how long

  • Nothing else: Colorwise sends no game statistics, crash reports or ad data, so there is nothing more to delete.
  • Once the phone apps are out, App Store and Google Play will keep their own record of anything you buy.

Data on your device

Everything the game saves in your browser or on your phone (scores, settings, Prisms and skins) is deleted when you clear the site’s data or uninstall the app.

Read the full Colorwise privacy policy, or email contact@deevolutionstudios.com with any question.
