Colorwise

Word or hue? Beat the beat. A neon rhythm game of colors that lie.

▶ Play on the web

About the Game

Colorwise is a neon rhythm game of colors that lie. Gates fly at you on the beat: swipe toward the color the word says and ignore the color it's painted. If that color isn't on the ring, tap. And when a gold ring appears, the rule flips.

  • Three tracks, a Daily challenge and a Practice mode that teaches every gate
  • Earn Prisms as you play and spend them on skins for the gate wheel, the tunnel and the sky
  • World leaderboards per track, weekly and all-time

Colorwise is coming to iPhone and Android. Until then, play it in your browser.
