D2048: Merge Dungeon

2048 goes dungeon crawling! Merge coins into gems and chests, outwit monsters and slay bosses in 60 levels.

About the Game

Swipe to slide the board and merge matching treasure: copper coins into silver, silver into gold, gold into gems and treasure chests. Every merge earns gold. But you're not alone down here. Skeletons and orcs appear without warning, and if your best treasure touches one, it runs off with it and hurts you on the way out!

How to play

  • Swipe to move every tile; matching treasures merge into the next one up
  • Each merge earns gold to spend on swords, hammers and potions
  • Skeletons steal silver and better, orcs steal gold and better
  • Two skeletons merge into an orc; two orcs summon the Sorcerer
  • Slimes land on empty squares and never move: only a hammer clears them

Adventure

Travel a map of 60 levels across six lands, from the Open Fields to the Dragon's Lair. Every tenth level from 20 on is a boss fight: a skeleton, the Orc War Chief, the Lich, the Wizard and finally the Dragon.

Fair and friendly

  • No forced ads: a short video only plays when you choose to watch one
  • Optional packs change how the game looks. They never change the game
  • No account needed, progress saved on your device, plays offline
