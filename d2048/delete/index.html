✦ Account & data deletion

Delete your D2048 data

D2048: Merge Dungeon · by De-Evolution Studios (DE-EVOLUTION STUDIOS LLC)

D2048 has no accounts: there is nothing to sign in to, and your progress is saved only on your own device.

How to ask for deletion

  1. To have your purchase records deleted (kept by our purchase service, RevenueCat, so you can restore packs), email contact@deevolutionstudios.com with the subject “D2048 data deletion”.
  2. If you can, include the date of your purchase and your App Store or Google Play order number, so we can find the record.
  3. We delete it and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your purchase history at RevenueCat (which packs, when, price and currency) and the random ID it is stored under

What is kept, and for how long

  • App Store and Google Play keep their own record of your purchases, under their own privacy policies.
  • Ad data held by Google can be reset or deleted in your device’s Google or Apple settings.
  • Anonymous game statistics aren’t linked to you and are deleted by Google after a limited time; crash reports after 90 days. Ask in the same email and we’ll ask Google to delete the statistics from your installation.

Data on your device

Your game progress lives only on your device: uninstalling D2048 deletes it.

Read the full D2048: Merge Dungeon privacy policy, or email contact@deevolutionstudios.com with any question.
