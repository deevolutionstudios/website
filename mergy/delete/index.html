✦ Account & data deletion

Delete your Mergy data

Mergy · by De-Evolution Studios (DE-EVOLUTION STUDIOS LLC)

Mergy has no accounts: there is nothing to sign in to, and we hold no data about you. Your merges and board are saved only on your own device.

How to ask for deletion

  1. If you have a question about your data, email contact@deevolutionstudios.com with the subject “Mergy privacy”.
  2. We answer within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Nothing is kept by us: Mergy sends no game statistics, crash reports or personal details to us.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Google Play (and later the App Store) keep their own record of anything you buy, under their own privacy policies.
  • Ad data held by Google can be reset or deleted in your device’s Google or Apple settings.

Data on your device

Your merges, board and settings live only on your device: uninstalling Mergy deletes them.

Read the full Mergy privacy policy, or email contact@deevolutionstudios.com with any question.
