Mergy

Drag. Merge. Relax. A cozy little merge game with Mergy the cat.

Listed as “Mergy” on Google Play, “Mergy: Cozy Merge” on the App Store.

About the Game

Mergy is a tiny, cozy merge game you can play forever. Curl up in a lamp-lit room with snow falling past the window, while Mergy the cat dozes on top of the board.

Two moods

  • Chill: every round fills the board with copies of one thing. Drag one onto its twin and they merge into the next: a cotton puff into thread, thread into a ball of yarn, all the way to a patchwork quilt, then on through tea and sleep. No timers, no levels, no losing: every merge adds one to your total.
  • Less Chill: sleepy animals lie along the top of the board, dreaming of what they'd like. Tap the rocking chair, the snoozing teapot and the napping goose for things, merge them up, and hand each customer what they're dreaming of.

What's inside

  • 36 cozy hand-painted items in three lines, knitting, tea and sleep, every step made from the one before
  • Soft merge notes and calm music, each with its own volume
  • The Cozy Hall of Fame: sign in with Google (or Apple on iPhone) to put your name and merges on the world board, this week and all time
  • Optional packs: the Halloween Pack and the Christmas Pack each bring 36 seasonal items and their own room
  • Remove ads: one small purchase and the ad is gone for good

Mergy is coming to Android first, then iPhone.
