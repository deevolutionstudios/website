Mergy

Drag. Merge. Relax. The coziest little merge game there is.

About the Game

Mergy is a tiny, cozy merge game you can play forever. Every round fills the board with copies of one thing: four strawberries, eight cupcakes, or now and then the whole board of sixteen. Drag one onto its twin and they merge into something new. Keep merging until one is left, and the next round begins from it.

  • Only twins merge, and every merge counts: watch your total climb
  • No timers, no levels, no losing: just you and the board
  • 162 hand-painted items to discover, from fruit and flowers to treasure and the sky
  • Optional board packs, like the Halloween Pack with 97 spooky items on a cracked-stone board
  • Sign in with Google (or Apple on iPhone) to put your name on the world high score board, this week and all time

Mergy is coming to Android first, then iPhone.
