✦ FAQ

Common
Questions.

What kinds of projects do you take on?

We work on mobile games, card games, board games, and digital projects. We also offer character design, story creation, game balancing, and creative consultation for other developers and studios. If it involves games, we're interested.

Do you work with first-time game creators?

Absolutely — that's one of our favorite things. If you have an idea and you're not sure where to start, we'll walk you through the whole process. No experience required. Just bring your excitement.

How does a project typically start?

Send us a message and we'll set up a call. We'll ask questions, listen, and figure out what you actually need. Then we put together a plan and go from there. It really is that simple.

Can you help if my game is already in development?

Yes — and we do it a lot. Whether you need fresh eyes on mechanics, help balancing systems, or someone to develop your story and characters mid-production, we can jump in at any stage.

Where are you based?

We're headquartered in Munich, Germany, with roots in Brooklyn, New York. We work with clients worldwide — time zones have never been a problem.
