Elementra

2 cards are all you need to win it all!

About the Game

Elementra: Dragon Academy is a dynamic card game that combines strategy, quick thinking, and a touch of anime flair. Designed for 2 to 8 players, this game offers engaging hand management mechanics that keep each session lively and unpredictable.

Players take on the roles of dragon riders competing in a high-stakes academy tournament. Each turn, you draw a card and decide which to keep and which to discard, triggering unique effects that can aid your strategy or hinder your opponents. The objective is to pair a rider with a dragon, optimizing their combined combat strength to outlast rivals.

The game's vibrant artwork and fast-paced gameplay make it a hit for both casual gatherings and dedicated game nights. Its blend of tactical decision-making and interactive play ensures that no two games are alike, offering endless replayability.

Whether you're a seasoned gamer or new to card games, Elementra: Dragon Academy provides an accessible yet challenging experience that will have you coming back for more.

Play it on your phone

Elementra is also an app: Elementra: Dragon Academy for iPhone, iPad and Android, with online matches against players around the world.
