Elementra: Dragon Academy

Bond a Rider and a Dragon, outwit your rivals and win fantasy card duels. Two cards are all you need.

▶ Play on the web

About the Game

Welcome, new recruit. Your training begins at the Elementra: Dragon Academy, where Riders and Dragons learn to fight as one.

Elementra is a fast fantasy card duel for 2 to 4 players. Each Round you hold just two cards, and you need a Rider and a Dragon to Battle. On your turn you draw a card, then discard one, and every discard triggers its ability. Peek at a rival's hand, swap cards, knock a classmate out of the Round, or protect yourself for one more turn.

Features

  • Quick Rounds of bluffing, deduction and timing
  • Nine Affinities, each with its own Rider and Dragon
  • Find a Match online, invite friends to private tables, or play the computer, even offline
  • Collect and upgrade your cards through five finishes, with foil and holographic shine
  • Daily and weekly quests, seasonal events and a friends list

Prefer cards on the table?

The app is the digital edition of Elementra, our tabletop card game for 2 to 8 players.
