This website

deevolutionstudios.com · Effective October 23, 2026

This notice covers the website deevolutionstudios.com, run by DE-EVOLUTION STUDIOS LLC ("we", "us"). The games (including their web versions at candi.deevolutionstudios.com and play.deevolutionstudios.com) are covered by their own policies above.

Visiting the site

The site has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics or tracking, and it sets no cookies. Our host, Hostinger, keeps standard server logs to deliver and secure the site: your IP address, the date and time, the page requested, and your browser's user agent. We use them only to run and protect the site. See Hostinger's privacy policy.

The fonts and images are served from this site itself; your browser doesn't contact Google Fonts or any other third party to show these pages. Store badges link to the App Store and Google Play, which have their own policies.

The contact form

If you send a message through the contact form, the name, email address, subject and message you enter are sent to us through Formspree, which delivers them to our inbox. We use them only to answer you, and delete them when they are no longer needed for that. See Formspree's privacy policy. If you email us directly, your email is handled by our email provider, Hostinger.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing contact@deevolutionstudios.com. If you live in the European Union or the UK, you can also object to how we use it and complain to a data protection authority.

Changes

If we change this notice, we'll update this page and the date above.
