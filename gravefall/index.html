Grave Fall

Run! Cuteness is Coming!

About the Game

Grave Fall is the game that started it all for De-Evolution Studios. In this fast-paced mobile escape, you play as Skully — a skeleton on the run from the most dangerous thing in the graveyard: Candi. She's sweet, she's adorable, and she's absolutely terrifying.

Your only goal? Dodge, dive, and fall as fast as you can to escape her relentless, candy-coated affection. The game's increasing difficulty and dynamic environment require quick thinking and precise movements, offering a challenging and engaging experience.

As De-Evolution Studios' inaugural title, Grave Fall laid the foundation for the company's commitment to creating fun, quick-to-learn, and challenging games. Its success paved the way for subsequent titles like Candi's Swamp Hop and Lil' Cthulhu, further establishing the studio's reputation in the gaming community.
