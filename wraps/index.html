Wraps & Isis

Escape from Giza — if you can!

About the Game

Wraps & Isis: Escape from Giza follows the adventure of Wraps, an awakened mummy trapped deep inside the Great Pyramid of Giza. Confused but determined, Wraps teams up with his loyal cat Isis and the mischievous ghost of Khufu — the pyramid's original occupant and now its restless spirit.

De-Evolution Studios was tasked with creating the story, characters, and character designs for this project — building a world full of personality, humor, and heart. Every detail was crafted from early sketches to final designs to bring this unlikely trio to life.

Together, Wraps, Isis, and Khufu solve puzzles, dodge ancient traps, and outsmart the pyramid's shifting walls as they fight their way to freedom. The game blends action, mythology, and charm — turning one mummy's escape into a fast-paced, character-driven adventure.

The world expanded in Wraps & Isis 2: Riddle of the Sphinx, a candy-crush-style puzzle game where players dive back in. Once again, De-Evolution Studios created the characters, story, and designs — giving players another fun way to explore this lighthearted world full of humor, magic, and adventure.
