Candi's Swamp Hop is out on Google Play

Candi's Swamp Hop is out now on Google Play. Hop Candi across a swamp full of skulls, slime and surprises, land on the moss for bonus points, and see how far you can go before the swamp gets you.

The iPhone version, Candi's Swamp Hop: Remastered, is with Apple for review, and we'll post here the moment it's out.
