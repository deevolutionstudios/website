Testers wanted: Mergy, our cozy merge game

Meet Mergy, a cozy little merge game, and Mergy the cat, who would very much like you to get comfortable. Drag two of the same thing together and they merge into something new: a cotton puff becomes yarn, yarn becomes a knitted scarf, and on it goes all the way to a patchwork quilt.

There are two ways to play. Chill is endless merging with nothing to lose, just you, the board and a warm blanket. Less Chill has sleepy animal customers dreaming of the things you make, so you merge to fill their orders. Your merges go on the world board, Legends of the Lounge, if you like a little friendly competition.

We're looking for testers on iPhone and Android before it comes out. You'll get the game early, play it whenever you like, and tell us what you think: what feels good, what's confusing, and anything that breaks.
