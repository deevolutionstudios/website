✦ Who We Are

Built by Players,
for Players.

Founded in 2012, De-Evolution Studios is a game development company originally based in Brooklyn, New York, and now headquartered in Munich, Germany.

At De-Evolution Studios, we live and breathe game design. We build worlds, characters, and stories that stay with you long after the game ends. Our team of designers, developers, storytellers, and artists share one focus — creating games that are easy to pick up but hard to put down.

Every project starts with the core mechanics. We craft systems that feel intuitive while offering real depth. Whether it's a clever card combo, a unique puzzle twist, or a finely tuned game loop, we're obsessed with balance and gameplay that just feels right.

We don't stop at our own projects. We help other creators and companies bring their ideas to life. Stuck on character design, world-building, story, or balance? We've got you.

Games are meant to be shared, explored, and enjoyed. That's why everything we create — from mobile apps to card games — is built to surprise, challenge, and entertain. We're here to make games fun again, for players and creators alike.

What We
Believe In.

🎮

Fun First, Always

If it's not genuinely fun to play, it's not done. We test obsessively, iterate relentlessly, and don't ship boring. Every game gets the full treatment — no shortcuts.

🌍

Anyone Can Play

We specialize in games that are quick to learn and challenging to master. Fun shouldn't be gated behind complexity. The best games invite everyone in.

❤️

Characters Matter

Characters drive great stories — and great games. Whether it's a hero you root for or a villain you love to hate, we focus on depth, personality, and real connection.

🤝

Collaboration Over Everything

Whether we're building our own games or helping someone else build theirs, we work as partners. Your vision stays yours — we just bring the skills to make it real.

⚖️

Balance is Everything

Great game design is about systems that feel fair, fun, and satisfying. We're obsessed with balance — every choice should feel meaningful and every mechanic should earn its place.

🚀

Always Shipping

Ideas are worthless without execution. We've shipped 7+ games across mobile, card, and board formats — and we're always building the next one. Done beats perfect.
